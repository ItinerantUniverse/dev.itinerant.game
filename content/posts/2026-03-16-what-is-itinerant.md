- A persistent, open-world planet operating in real time.
- Ability to add multiple planets and systems operating from many different servers.
- No 2D overlays/HUDs/hard-codded interfaces - everything works and runs from within the 3D game engine.
- Planet rendering must support multiple layers of geology and 'holes'/overhangs in the ground.
